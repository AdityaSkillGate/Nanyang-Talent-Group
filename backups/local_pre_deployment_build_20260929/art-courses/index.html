Chinese Calligraphy

Five Traditional Scripts & Classical Art Appreciation

Chinese calligraphy (shufa) is one of the highest forms of traditional Chinese visual art, conveying thoughts while showcasing the abstract beauty of line and rhythm. The course covers the five classical scripts: Kaishu, Lishu, Xingshu, Caoshu, and Zhuanshu, along with appreciation of historical copybooks and stone inscriptions.

Kaishu (Regular Script) - Structure & BalanceLishu (Official Script) - Silkworm Head & Swallow TailXingshu & Caoshu (Running & Cursive) - Flow & Rhythm
S$ 50 (2 hrs)

Chinese Painting

Bird-and-Flower, Landscape, Xieyi and Gongbi

A comprehensive study of Chinese traditional painting across two millennia of artistic heritage. Classified by genres (Bird-and-flower, Landscape, Figure) and methods including Xieyi (freehand style), Gongbi (fine-brush detail), and Pomo (splash-ink), expressing inner character through ink and brushwork.
